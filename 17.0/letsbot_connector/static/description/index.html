LetsBot WhatsApp Connector

Answer customers on WhatsApp with AI that knows your Odoo orders, invoices, deliveries and products.

Why LetsBot for Odoo

  • One-click connection. Press Connect to LetsBot in Settings. Odoo creates a dedicated integration user and a standard API key and hands it to LetsBot over a secure server-to-server call. Nothing to copy, nothing to paste.
  • Real-time updates. When a sales order, customer invoice, delivery or CRM lead changes, LetsBot is notified within seconds, so the WhatsApp assistant always answers with the current status.
  • Signed and private by design. Every webhook is signed with HMAC-SHA256 and only carries the record id, its last update time and its status. Customer data is never pushed; LetsBot reads what it needs through the Odoo API with the permissions you control.
  • Never slows Odoo down. Notifications are queued inside your transaction and delivered by a scheduled action after it commits, with automatic retries and back-off.
  • WhatsApp from the contact form. A WhatsApp (LetsBot) button opens the customer's conversation in LetsBot.
  • Disconnect any time. One click revokes the API key and stops all notifications.

What LetsBot does with Odoo

  • AI replies on WhatsApp about products, prices, stock, order status, invoices and deliveries.
  • Order, payment and shipping notifications to your customers on WhatsApp.
  • Leads and contacts created from WhatsApp conversations, with a summary in the chatter.
  • Arabic, English, Spanish and Portuguese out of the box.

Setup in 3 steps

  1. Install the app.
  2. Open Settings → LetsBot WhatsApp and press Connect to LetsBot.
  3. Sign in to LetsBot (or create a free account) and choose your workspace. Done.

Requirements: a LetsBot account (letsbot.net) and outgoing HTTPS access from your Odoo server. Works with Odoo Community and Enterprise, on-premise and Odoo.sh. Note: the dedicated integration user is an internal user; on plans billed per user you can instead pick an existing user under Advanced.
